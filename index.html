Reactivity & Reactions:
Reacts vigorously with cold water: 2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂↑. Stored under kerosene oil.
Real-World Applications:
Table salt (NaCl), baking soda (NaHCO₃), street lamps, nuclear reactor coolant.
Discovery & Historical Note:
Isolated in 1807 by Sir Humphry Davy via electrolysis of molten NaOH.
